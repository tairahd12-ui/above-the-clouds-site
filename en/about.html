About

Less noise. More meaning. Visuals that make you think.

Founder / Video Creator

Taira Nagade (Tyler)

At 17, I moved to Vancouver, Canada on my own and experienced life abroad. After coming back to Japan, I got into video and music early — shooting and editing for YouTube, running a channel, and live-streaming from clubs.

Time spent in Australia shaped how I think about the work: it's not just about making video, but about using video to move people.

Today I work mainly on fashion video for brands like KEBOZ, TIMBERLAND, G-SHOCK, NIKE, and Onitsuka Tiger, handling everything from planning and shooting to editing and color grading. I also make music, event, and tourism content, and I'm working to grow above the clouds from a video studio into a creative brand connecting music, fashion, travel, and culture.
